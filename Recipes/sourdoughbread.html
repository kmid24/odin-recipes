Sourdough bread

Easy overnight sourdough bread!

Ingredients

  • 100g active sourdough starter
  • 400g water
  • 500g bread flour
  • 11g salt

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, mix together the starter and water until mostly combined.
  2. Add the bread flour and salt. Mix until no dry flour remains.
  3. Let the dough rest for 30 minutes.
  4. Perform one round of stretch and folds. Cover and let rest for 30 minutes. Repeat this process every 30 minutes for a total of 3 rounds.
  5. Cover the dough and let it bulk ferment for about 8 hours, or until it has risen noticeably and looks light and airy.
  6. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and shape into a tight loaf.
  7. Place the dough seam-side up in a floured proofing basket or bowl lined with a floured towel.
  8. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
  9. The next morning, place your Dutch oven in the oven and preheat to 450°F (232°C).
  10. Turn the dough onto parchment paper, score the top, and carefully transfer it to the hot Dutch oven.
  11. Bake for 4 mins covered at 450°F. Then reduce the oven to 435°F (224°C) and bake 20 minutes covered. Lastly,remove the lid and bake 8–10 minutes more, or until deep golden brown.
  12. Cool completely on a wire rack before slicing!

Notes

  • Bulk fermentation time will vary depending on the temperature of your kitchen. Look for dough that has risen, feels airy, and jiggles slightly when the bowl is gently shaken.
  • For best results, use an active starter that has doubled in size before mixing your dough.
