Apple Puff Pasteries

Flaky, golden puff pastry filled with warm cinnamon apples and a little brown sugar.

Ingredients

  • 4 small apples, peeled and diced
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp all-purpose flour or cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp water

For the pastries

  • 1 package frozen puff pastry, thawed (usually 2 sheets)
  • 1 egg yolk plus a little egg white, beaten (for egg wash)

Instructions

  1. Add the diced apples, brown sugar, cinnamon, flour or cornstarch, and water to a pan. Cook over medium heat for about 10 minutes, stirring frequently, until the apples soften and the filling thickens. Set aside to cool.
  2. Preheat the oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Cut the thawed puff pastry into rectangles. Make slits on one side.
  3. Fill the pockets: Spoon the cooled apple filling onto one side of each rectangle, leaving space around the edges. Fold the pastry over the filling and press the edges together with a fork to seal.
  4. Brush with the beaten egg.
  5. Place on the prepared baking sheet and bake for 12 minutes. Then increase the heat to 420°F and bake another 4–5 minutes, until puffed and golden brown.
  6. Let cool for a few minutes before serving. Enjoy warm!
